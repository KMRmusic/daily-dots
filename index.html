How to play

Hear the melody, then find it in five tries.

  • Press play to hear the five-note melody. You get three listens, or one in Expert mode.
  • After listening you have 15 seconds to explore on the keyboard. Press Ready if you’re done early.
  • Then play your five notes into the row and press Enter. The tiles change colour to show how close you were.
  • Each new attempt starts with another 15 seconds to explore.
  • Octave matters: C4 and C5 are different notes.
  • Expert mode: 8 seconds to explore, and the keys are silent while you enter notes. One hint per game gives you another listen, but costs two rows.
