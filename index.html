Daily Dots

🔊 No sound? Turn your volume up. On an iPhone, check the silent switch too.

Laptop keys: A to K play C to C, with W, E, T, Y and U for the black keys. Z and X change octave.

How to play

Hear the melody, then find the notes.

  • Press play to hear the five-note melody. How many listens you get depends on the difficulty.
  • After listening you get time to explore on the keyboard. Press Ready if you’re done early.
  • Then play your five notes into the row and press Enter. The tiles change colour to show how close you were.
  • Octave matters: C4 and C5 are different notes. A ↑ or ↓ on a grey tile means right note, wrong octave.
  • Daily is the same melody for everyone, once a day. Practice is as many melodies as you like.
  • Four difficulty levels, named after dynamics: Piano, Mezzo, Forte and Fortissimo. Change it in Settings.
  • Scoring: 100 points for solving on your last attempt, plus 100 for each attempt left and 50 for each listen you didn’t use. Mezzo doubles your score, Forte triples it and Fortissimo quadruples it.
  • At Forte, one hint per game gives you another listen, but costs two attempts and 100 points.
  • On a laptop, the A to K keys play the keyboard.
